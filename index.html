How to build your CV

A quick walkthrough of everything in the editor. Reopen it anytime from How to.

  1. 01

    Start with your basics

    Fill in your name, title, email, phone, location and website in Basics. To add a photo, click Upload photo, then drag to move it and use the slider to zoom. Choose a circle or square and press Apply. You can change this later with Adjust crop, or hide the photo with the toggle.

  2. 02

    Add and organise your sections

    The bar at the top of the editor lists every section. Click a name to jump straight to it. Use + Add to add an entry, ↑ ↓ to reorder, ⧉ to duplicate and ✕ to delete. Click an entry heading to fold it away, or use ⇕ to fold a whole section. At the bottom, Add a section gives you ready-made ones such as Certifications, Publications, Awards, Volunteering, Teaching and References. The eye icon hides a section from your CV.

  3. 03

    Improve your writing with AI

    Text boxes where you write sentences have an AI chip. Click it to get a cleaned-up version and tips to make it stronger. Each job also has a Rewrite highlights with STAR button, which turns your bullets into achievements (Situation, Task, Action, Result). The AI needs a free key, see the last step.

  4. 04

    Choose a design

    Open the Design tab. Every template is shown with your own CV, so you can compare them. There are nine, including Europass for European applications and Plain ATS, a simple black and white layout that online job portals read easily. You can also change the accent colour, font, text size, spacing, margins and paper size (A4 or US Letter).

  5. 05

    Check the pages

    The preview shows a dashed line where each new page starts, and the bar above it shows roughly how many pages you have. Use Fit and the + / − buttons to zoom. Drag the divider between the editor and the preview to make either side wider. Made a mistake? The Undo arrows next to Saved (or Ctrl+Z) bring it back, even after Clear.

  6. 06

    Download and back up

    Click Download PDF. In the window that opens, choose Save as PDF and turn on Background graphics, or the coloured parts will be missing. Your CV is saved only in this browser, so use Export now and then to keep a backup file, and Import to load it again, for example on another computer. On a small screen these are in the ⋯ menu.

  7. 07

    About the AI key

    AI features use OpenRouter. Paste a free key from openrouter.ai/keys below to switch them on. It is stored only in this browser and sent only to OpenRouter. Everything else in the app works without it.

    No key set. AI features are off until you add one.
